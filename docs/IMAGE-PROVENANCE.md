# Image provenance

All eighteen destination and activity images were generated with the built-in image generation tool on **20 October 2026**. No stock photography or scraped destination photographs are used.

Six originals were created during the provisional branding stage and reused for Miles & Moments. Twelve additional scenes were generated for this implementation. Original prompt records are preserved in [IMAGE-PROMPTS.md](IMAGE-PROMPTS.md) and [ACTIVITY-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md](ACTIVITY-IMAGE-PROMPTS.md).

Files follow `destination.webp`, `destination-1.webp`, `destination-2.webp` inside `dist/assets/images`. The eighteen shipped WebP files total approximately 6.34 MB. Images are optimized with sharp, limited to 1536px width at quality 83. The original generated PNGs are kept locally and excluded from Git.

These are illustrative generated impressions, not documentary evidence, real hotel photography, verified attractions, or guaranteed activities. They may contain visual or geographic inaccuracies. The UI labels this distinction in the footer and relevant booking/gallery views.

The compass favicon and interface icons are original code-authored SVGs. Fonts are third-party typefaces under the accompanying SIL Open Font licenses; they are not represented as original photography or original font designs.
